# French Reading Rules: deck content

The curated content for `plan-french-reading-rules-deck.md`. One row per rule, in deck order.
`key` is the ASCII stem of the rule's audio files (`<key>-1.mp3` .. `<key>-3.mp3`). Each rule
also gets a fixed, hand-generated lowercase UUIDv4 `id` in the data file, written once and never
changed (it is the note's `RuleId` and the media name's id).

Transcriptions are IPA between slashes. `∅` means the letters are silent. `Note` is shown on the
back only; empty means none.
